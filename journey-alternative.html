A Journey app alternative built around AI

Journey is the journaling app for people who write from more than one device: iPhone, Android, Windows, Mac, the web, even Chrome OS, with maps, weather tagging, and guided coach programs along the way. What it isn't built around is a model that reads what you write. MirrorNotes is: daily personalized prompts, mood analysis, weekly digests, and a journal you can ask questions, all running on your iPhone, never in the cloud, and completely free.

TL;DR

  • Journey = the most cross-platform journal, with maps, weather, and guided programs. MirrorNotes = a journal that talks back.
  • MirrorNotes AI is fully on-device (Gemma 3): prompts, digests, and "Ask your journal" work offline and entries never reach a server.
  • MirrorNotes is free. Journey's cross-platform membership runs about $29.99/yr (or a one-time Premium license per platform).
  • Write from five different devices and want maps of everywhere you've journaled? Stay with Journey. Want insight from what you write, for free? Try MirrorNotes.

Different jobs: everywhere-journal vs. mirror

Journey's strength is that it goes wherever you go. Write on your iPhone at breakfast, pick it up on Windows at your desk, review it on the web from someone else's laptop. Automatic weather and location tagging, an atlas map of everywhere you've written, guided coach programs on gratitude and self-care, and solid export options make it a genuinely capable, long-running journal. If cross-device breadth and a mapped life are what you want, Journey is a strong, honest choice, and we won't pretend otherwise.

MirrorNotes is built for a different job: reflection, on one device, with a model that actually reads what you wrote and gives something back:

  • Daily Nudge: tomorrow morning's prompt is generated from what you've actually been writing about, not a fixed coach-program script.
  • Ask your journal: "when do I feel most alive?" "what am I avoiding?" Answered from months of your own entries.
  • Weekly Digest: every Sunday, the week's themes and moods summarized.
  • Mood Timeline: emotional trends you'd never notice reading day by day, with alerts when several entries in a row trend negative.
The privacy story is similar, but not identical: Journey offers optional end-to-end encryption for your synced entries across its many platforms. MirrorNotes takes a narrower approach: there's no cross-platform sync to secure in the first place, because the AI and your writing never leave your iPhone's Neural Engine. Fewer devices, but nothing to intercept in transit, ever.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use both?

Sure. Journey as the cross-platform archive with maps and photos; MirrorNotes as the daily writing and reflection habit on your iPhone. MirrorNotes is free and unlimited, so trying it costs nothing.

Can I import my Journey entries?

Not yet, import is on the roadmap. New entries you write in MirrorNotes are yours: stored on-device in SwiftData and synced to your own iCloud.

Why iPhone only?

The on-device AI is the product. Modern iPhone chips run Gemma 3 well; spreading thin across platforms would mean either cloud AI (breaking the privacy guarantee) or a worse experience. iPhone-first is a deliberate trade.
