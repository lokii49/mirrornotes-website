What these numbers are

This page shows totals only. Nothing here can be traced back to a person, a device or a timer.

What's counted

  • App sessions, devices with a session, first-time installs and App Clip card views, as Apple reports them
  • Whether each request for a shared link was a link preview or an open in a browser

What's never kept

  • Timer names, times or the links themselves
  • IP addresses, devices or user agents
  • Anything from inside the app — it sends nothing to us

Why some numbers are missing or low

Apple's figures include only people who agreed to share analytics with developers, and Apple leaves out any figure from fewer than five people. Those days show as a dotted line, not zero. Apple reports each day a day or two late and can revise it for five days. Apple splits each day by device type, country, app version and how the app was opened, and the device figure adds those up. A phone that appears in two of them, such as before and after an update, counts twice. Treat it as an upper bound, not a count of people.

Link counts miss every link opened by someone who has Timer, because the link opens straight in the app without asking our server, and links sent before Timer 1.0.5, which use the old lokii49.github.io address. The counts are kept for three months. Read the privacy policy.
