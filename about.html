About MirrorNotes

MirrorNotes (sometimes written Mirror Notes) is a private AI journaling app for iPhone. You write; an AI that runs on your iPhone reflects your own words back to you. Your journal text never leaves your device.

MirrorNotes at a glance

  • An iPhone journaling app, made by one independent developer
  • All AI runs on your iPhone: Apple's Foundation Models on Apple Intelligence-eligible iPhones, a bundled Gemma 3 model on the rest
  • No account, no sign-in, no MirrorNotes server that receives your journal
  • Free to write, forever. AI features are free until 15 November 2026, then part of the Core and Deep plans
  • The app's source code is public under the AGPL-3.0 license

Who makes it

MirrorNotes is designed, built and supported by Lokesh Pudhari, a solo developer. When you email support, the person who wrote the app reads it.

Why it exists

Most apps promise they won't misuse your data. A promise about intent can change with a new owner, a funding crunch or an updated terms of service. MirrorNotes is built so that it can't: your entries stay on your iPhone, so there is nothing to sell, leak or hand over, whoever runs the company next year.

The longer version is in Why I don't want your data.

How it makes money

A plain subscription through Apple's In-App Purchase for the AI features. Because the AI runs on your phone, there is no cloud AI bill to cover, so there are no ads, no data deals and no reason to read your journal. Writing, history, search and iCloud backup stay free.

Check it yourself

You don't have to take our word for any of this. The app's source code is public on GitHub under the AGPL-3.0 license, and you can run a network monitor while you write: no journal text is sent to us or to any AI service. iCloud backup, if you use it, goes to your own iCloud account. The privacy policy lists exactly what does and doesn't leave your device.
