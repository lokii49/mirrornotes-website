The AI journal app that keeps its brain on your phone

Most AI journal apps work the same way: you write, your entry is sent to a server, and a cloud model (often OpenAI's) generates a prompt or summary back. What makes an app an "AI journal app"?

The label covers a wide range of apps, but the common thread is that the app does more than store text: it reads what you wrote and generates something back, a prompt, a mood score, a summary, an answer to a question. That reading step is where the apps differ most.

Cloud-based AI journal apps send your entry to a server running a large model (frequently a third-party API like OpenAI's), get a response, and display it. That's simple to build and produces strong results, but it means your private writing is transmitted and processed off-device, often by a company that isn't the one you downloaded the app from.

MirrorNotes takes the on-device route. It uses Apple's own Foundation Models on Apple Intelligence-eligible iPhones, and its own bundled Gemma 3 model everywhere else. Both are smaller than the cloud giants, but tuned for exactly this job: reading your own entries and reflecting them back, without a network call.

Frequently asked questions

Is on-device AI as good as cloud AI?

For general-purpose tasks, large cloud models are more capable. For a narrow job, reading your own entries and reflecting patterns back, a well-tuned on-device model, Apple's Foundation Models or MirrorNotes' own Gemma 3, does the job without the round trip, the server cost, or the privacy trade-off.

Do I need an account to use the AI features?

No. There's no sign-in and no account. Open the app and write; the AI features unlock automatically once there's enough of your own writing to draw from.

Can I turn the AI off?

You can simply ignore the prompts and Ask feature and use MirrorNotes as a plain journal. Nothing about the AI is required to write and keep entries.

Is there a free trial or paywall?

Not until 15 November 2026. Until then every AI feature is free. From then, Daily Reflection, Ask, Weekly Digest and Mood Timeline come in the Core and Deep plans (7-day free trial for new subscribers); writing, your history, search and iCloud backup stay free forever.
