Before a reflection is shown, the app checks that the quoted sentence really is in the entry. If it can't find one, you see that honestly instead of invented detail. On iPhones with Apple Intelligence this runs on Apple's Foundation Models; on every other supported iPhone, on the bundled Gemma 3 model. Both on the phone, neither online.

Your journal text cannot leave your phone. There is no server to send it to.

Why is it free?

Most journaling apps pay for cloud AI on every entry you write, so they have to charge you, or worse, monetize you. MirrorNotes runs its AI on the chip already in your pocket. Our marginal cost per user is zero, so early access is too.

  • We can't sell your data. It never reaches us. There is nothing on our side to sell.
  • We can't lock your history. Your entries live in your iCloud, under your Apple ID.
  • We'll never charge you to read your own words. If paid tiers come later, they gate new AI features, not your archive.

No account. No card. Cancel is a non-issue: there's nothing to cancel.
