An offline journal where even the AI works in airplane mode

Plenty of journal apps let you write offline. MirrorNotes goes further: the AI itself, daily prompts, mood analysis, weekly summaries, questions over your entries, runs on your iPhone's chip, no connection required. On a flight, on a trail, underground: everything works.

TL;DR

  • Every feature works offline: writing, voice transcription, and all AI.
  • The model lives on your iPhone — Apple's Foundation Models on Apple Intelligence-eligible iPhones, or the bundled Gemma 3 1B model via CoreML otherwise. No API calls, ever.
  • Offline AI doubles as a privacy guarantee: text that never needs the internet never crosses it.
  • Optional iCloud sync to your own Apple ID when you're back online. No account, no sign-in.

"Offline" usually has an asterisk

Most journaling apps are offline the way a web browser is offline: you can look at what's cached, and writing queues up for later. But the moment AI is involved, the asterisk appears: prompts, insights, and summaries come from a server, so they need a connection. Your "offline journal" quietly becomes an online service with a local cache.

MirrorNotes has no asterisk because there is no server:

Offline-first is privacy-first

These two properties are the same engineering decision. An AI that works in airplane mode is an AI that demonstrably doesn't send your words anywhere. You can verify it yourself by flipping the switch and watching every feature keep working.

  • Entries are stored in SwiftData on your iPhone.
  • AI inference happens in your phone's memory, on your iPhone's own chip. The model reads your entry, generates the insight, and nothing is transmitted.
  • No account exists. There's nothing to sign into and no server-side profile of you.
  • When you're back online, optional CloudKit sync copies entries to your own iCloud, under your Apple ID, inaccessible to us.
Try the test: put your phone in airplane mode, write an entry, and ask your journal a question. It answers. That's the whole privacy policy, demonstrated in ten seconds. Details in our privacy policy.

Frequently asked questions

How big is the AI model? Does it eat my storage or battery?

On Apple Intelligence-eligible iPhones, MirrorNotes uses Apple's Foundation Models, already built into iOS. Everywhere else it falls back to Gemma 3 1B, a small model by modern standards, designed for exactly this kind of on-device use. Either way, inference runs on the Neural Engine, generates a short insight, and stops; nothing runs in the background. Insights are cached so nothing is regenerated within 24 hours.

What iPhone do I need?

MirrorNotes requires iOS 17 or later. On iPhones that also support Apple Intelligence (iOS 26+, Apple Intelligence turned on), MirrorNotes uses Apple's own on-device model; every other supported iPhone uses the bundled Gemma 3 model. Recent iPhones run either model comfortably; newer chips are simply faster.

If I lose my phone, are my entries gone?

Enable iCloud sync and your entries are backed up to your own Apple ID and restorable on a new device. Without sync, entries exist only on the device: maximum privacy, your call.
