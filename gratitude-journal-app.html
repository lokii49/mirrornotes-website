Gratitude Journaling

A gratitude journal that notices what you're actually thankful for

Most gratitude journal apps hand you the same fixed prompt every day: "list three things you're grateful for." MirrorNotes is a general journal whose on-device AI reads your own recent entries and nudges you toward gratitude when it fits, not on a fixed script, plus mood tracking and the ability to ask your own journal what you've been grateful for over time.

TL;DR

  • MirrorNotes isn't a single-purpose gratitude app: it's a full journal, so gratitude entries sit alongside everything else you write, searchable together.
  • The Daily Nudge is generated from your own recent writing by an on-device AI, so it can point toward gratitude, a difficult day, or a pattern worth noticing, whichever fits.
  • Ask your journal lets you query your own gratitude history directly, for example "what have I been grateful for this month?"
  • Everything runs on your iPhone (Gemma 3, on-device), so your reflections never leave your device, and the app works fully offline.
  • Free and unlimited: no subscription tier, no entry cap, no account required.

Why a fixed gratitude prompt isn't always the right prompt

Dedicated gratitude journal apps are built around a template: three good things, every day, no variation. That works well for building a habit, but it treats every day the same, whether you had a genuinely great week or you're struggling and gratitude feels forced.

MirrorNotes takes a different approach. Because the AI reads what you've actually been writing, the Daily Nudge adapts: some days it surfaces something to be grateful for that you mentioned in passing three entries ago; other days it asks about something else entirely. Gratitude becomes one thread in an ongoing conversation with your own writing, not a daily form to fill out.

You can still journal gratitude directly. Write "three things I'm grateful for today" any time you want, MirrorNotes doesn't require the AI prompts to journal. The Daily Nudge is there when you want a starting point, not a requirement.

Frequently asked questions

What makes MirrorNotes a good gratitude journal app?

It isn't a gratitude-only app with fixed prompts. It's a general journal where the on-device AI generates a Daily Nudge from your own recent writing, so gratitude prompts show up when your entries suggest they'd help, alongside mood tracking and a searchable history of everything you're thankful for.

Is MirrorNotes free to use as a gratitude journal?

Yes. Every feature, unlimited entries, Daily Nudge, Ask your journal, Weekly Digest, Mood Timeline, is free with no subscription, trial, or paywall.

Can I look back at everything I've been grateful for?

Yes. Every entry is saved and searchable, and you can ask your journal directly, for example "what have I been grateful for this year?", and get an answer pulled from your own past entries, generated on-device.

Does gratitude journaling in MirrorNotes require an internet connection?

No. Because the AI (Gemma 3) runs on your iPhone's Neural Engine rather than a server, writing entries and generating prompts both work fully offline.
