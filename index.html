Architecture

Built different, on purpose.

Most AI apps send your data to a server to process it. We decided early that was a non-starter for a journal app. Your most personal thoughts deserve better.

So we rebuilt from the ground up. Gemma 3 — a capable 1-billion-parameter model — runs on your iPhone's Neural Engine. Inference is fast, free, and entirely local.

Entries sync to your iCloud via Apple's CloudKit — private to your Apple ID, not to us. We don't have access. We use Supabase only for authentication and subscription status — never journal data.

🧬

Gemma 3 1B on Neural Engine

Google's open-weight model, quantized for mobile. Runs in under a second per inference.

☁️

CloudKit — your iCloud, only

Apple's private sync. Only you can read it. Not even we can.

🔑

Sign In with Apple

No username or password to lose. Apple handles identity. We store nothing extra.
